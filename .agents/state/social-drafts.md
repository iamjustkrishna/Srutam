#### 3. LinkedIn Launch Post
```text
Most voice memo apps are still operating like it is 2012: you record an audio file, it sits in an endless list of "Recording_04.m4a", and you never listen to it again.

The newer wave of AI note apps swung in the other direction: forcing you into $15/month subscriptions while shipping your raw audio to third-party cloud servers.

I wanted something different for my own daily workflow: an on-device, zero-subscription tool that captures thoughts instantaneously and turns them into structured action items without breaking my focus.

Today, I am releasing Srutam 2.0.

Here is what we engineered into this release:

1. Instant Edge-Docked Capture
By the time you unlock your phone, swipe to an app, and tap record, your thought has already evaporated. Srutam features a lightweight overlay handle docked flush against your screen bezel. A single tap summons a mic from any screen, captures the thought, and collapses back without interrupting what you were doing.

2. On-Device Speech-to-Text
Your voice notes are your most private thoughts. Srutam runs on-device Sherpa-ONNX models locally. It transcribes audio directly on your phone hardware with zero cloud dependencies, zero data leakage, and zero recurring subscription fees.

3. Structured Insights: Next Steps, Key Ideas, and Decisions
Instead of drowning you in a giant wall of unedited transcription, Srutam parses each voice note into first-class Room database models:
- Next Steps: Action items with interactive checkboxes and direct transcript quotes.
- Key Ideas: Highlighted concept cards capturing the essence of your brainstorm.
- Decisions: Explicit records of what was decided and why.

4. Resilient Background Architecture
Voice notes cannot be lost if you switch apps or lock your screen. The processing pipeline is built on Android WorkManager with foreground sync services, ensuring multi-minute recordings process reliably in the background.

5. Adaptive Screen Matrix
Every screen has been tested across 5 form factors (Compact Phone, Standard Phone, Foldable, 7" Tablet, and 10" Tablet) using headless Roborazzi Native Graphics on the JVM.

Srutam is free, private, and built for builders, researchers, and thinkers who value their time and data privacy.

Check out the demo below and grab the build on GitHub / Play Store.

#AndroidDev #JetpackCompose #OnDeviceAI #OpenSource #Productivity #Kotlin #BuildInPublic
```
