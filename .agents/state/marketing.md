# Srutam Marketing & Conversion Engine

## Positioning & Core Hook
Srutam is the zero-friction offline audio note app for Android. 
Double-press the volume down button to record immediately without unlocking your phone or breaking your flow.

## The 4 Core Value Pillars (Relief Angles)
1. **Frictionless Hardware Trigger**:
   - Double-click Volume Down to record. Screen can stay black.
   - Eliminates the 3-step barrier (unlock phone -> find app -> press record) that causes lost thoughts.
2. **100% Offline Speech-to-Text**:
   - Local on-device Sherpa-ONNX model.
   - Zero cloud processing, zero data leaks, zero audio uploads.
3. **No Subscription Fatigue**:
   - Built to work without monthly paywalls or cloud lock-in.
4. **AI Summaries & Audio Chat**:
   - Get bulleted action items, key points, and ask questions about your recording.

## Target Audience
- Developers, founders, writers, and students who think faster than they type.
- People on walks, commutes, or in deep work who need to capture ideas in < 1 second.
- Privacy-conscious users tired of voice recorders that sync to private servers.
