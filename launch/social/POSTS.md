### Posting tips
- **X:** upload natively (don't post a YouTube link), and pin the thread. Post when developers are online, 8–10am PT on a weekday.
- **Instagram:** cross-post the Reel to Stories with a link sticker. The first 3 seconds decide reach, so don't trim the POV hook.
- **LinkedIn:** upload natively in the 4:5 crop and put the link in the first comment if you want more reach. Reply to every comment in the first hour.
