## 2. App Name Analysis & Alternatives

Google Play enforces a strict 30-character limit on the app title. Top apps utilize two distinct strategies:

1. **Global Monopoly Brands (WhatsApp, Snapchat, Facebook)**:
   - They use only the brand name (`Snapchat`) or brand + generic category (`WhatsApp Messenger`).
   - Why? They already have billions of monthly searches for the brand keyword alone.
2. **Breakout Challenger Apps (Notion, Otter, Evernote, Forest)**:
   - Formula: `[Brand]: [High Intent Primary Keyword 1] & [Keyword 2]`.
   - Google Play indexes the title with 2x to 3x higher weight than any other field.
   - Using only "Srutam" means forfeiting organic search visibility for high-volume terms like "voice notes", "audio notes", "transcribe", and "action items".

### Tested Title Options:
- **Option 1 (Recommended - Highest Search & Intent Balance)**:
  `Srutam: AI Voice Notes & Tasks` (30 chars)
- **Option 2 (Audio & Memo Focus)**:
  `Srutam: AI Voice Memo & Notes` (29 chars)
- **Option 3 (Transcription Focus)**:
  `Srutam: AI Voice Transcribe` (27 chars)
- **Option 4 (Audio Recorder Focus)**:
  `Srutam: Voice Notes & Audio` (27 chars)

## 4. Full Description (Copy-Paste Ready for Google Play Console)

```text
Stop letting your best ideas disappear into endless audio files you never listen to again.

Srutam is the modern AI voice recorder and note-taking app designed to turn raw spoken thoughts into clean, structured notes, action items, and clear summaries. Whether you are brainstorming on a walk, recording a meeting, capturing lecture takeaways, or doing a fast morning brain dump, Srutam listens, transcribes, and extracts what actually matters.

No manual re-typing. No messy voice memos. Just speak, and Srutam takes care of the rest.

KEY FEATURES

• One-Tap Voice Recording
Capture audio with instant live waveforms, responsive audio playback, and clean seek controls. Pause, resume, and organize your recordings effortlessly.

• Private On-Device Transcription
Transcribe speech to text with cutting-edge local AI models (powered by Sherpa ONNX Whisper). Your audio can be transcribed directly on your phone without sending sensitive voice recordings to remote servers.

• Automated AI Summaries & Key Points
Get clear, structured bullet points from any voice note. Srutam automatically organizes your thoughts into concise summaries, key topics, and digestible overviews.

• Instant Action Items & Checklist
Never miss a follow-up. Srutam detects tasks, commitments, and deadlines mentioned in your audio and formats them into an actionable checklist with checkable items.

• AI Copilot Chat
Have questions about what was said? Chat directly with your voice notes using AI. Ask for a brief summary, draft an email based on the recording, or pull specific numbers and decisions from past conversations.

• Smart Search & Categorical Hubs
Find any note in seconds. Search through transcribed text, titles, and generated tags. Filter your notes by automatically organized categories.

• Clean, Modern Material Design
Built with Jetpack Compose following modern Material 3 aesthetics. Enjoy smooth fluid animations, dark mode support, and an edge-to-edge experience tailored for Android.

• Privacy First by Design
Your voice notes and personal data stay under your control. Transcribe locally on your device, store notes in a local database, and configure your own API keys when using cloud AI features. Zero unwanted tracking.

WHO USES SRUTAM?

• Founders & Creators: Capture raw product ideas, content hooks, and strategy thoughts while walking or driving.
• Engineers & Builders: Record quick architectural thoughts, standup updates, and bug notes without losing focus.
• Students & Researchers: Record lectures, seminars, and study groups, then turn them into clear revision notes and study checklists.
• Busy Professionals: Turn 30-minute meetings and quick voice updates into crisp action items you can share immediately.
• ADHD & Brain Dumpers: Speak freely without organizing your thoughts first. Let AI structure the chaos into actionable clarity.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Tap Record and speak naturally.
2. Srutam records your audio with real-time waveform visualization.
3. Transcribe speech to text using offline or cloud AI.
4. Review your AI-generated summary, highlighted topics, and action checklist.
5. Search, edit, check off tasks, or chat with your note anytime.

Experience the future of voice notes. Download Srutam today and turn your spoken ideas into actionable progress.
```
